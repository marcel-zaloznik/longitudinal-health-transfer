# Third-party attribution

The STraTS and GRU-D implementations adapt the following methods and public implementations for irregular longitudinal multi-target regression. They are not official implementations.

**STraTS:** Sindhura Tipirneni and Chandan K. Reddy. *Self-Supervised Transformer for Sparse and Irregularly Sampled Multivariate Clinical Time-Series* (2022). Reference implementation: https://github.com/sindhura97/STraTS.

**GRU-D:** Zhengping Che, Sanjay Purushotham, Kyunghyun Cho, David Sontag and Yan Liu. *Recurrent Neural Networks for Multivariate Time Series with Missing Values* (2018). Reference implementation: https://github.com/PeterChe1990/GRU-D.
